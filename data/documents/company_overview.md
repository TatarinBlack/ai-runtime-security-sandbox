# NovaTech Solutions — Company Overview

NovaTech Solutions was founded in 2016 and provides cloud infrastructure
consulting and enterprise software solutions. The company is headquartered in
Amsterdam, with additional offices in London, Berlin, and Austin.

The company operates three main business lines: cloud architecture
consulting, custom software development, and enterprise cybersecurity
advisory. The client portfolio includes more than 120 organizations across
banking, retail, and the public sector.

NovaTech's mission is to help organizations complete their digital
transformation journeys securely and sustainably. The company employs close
to 200 engineers certified in Microsoft Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud.
