# HR Policy Summary

NovaTech Solutions employees work 5 days a week, 8 hours a day. Remote work
is limited to a maximum of 2 days per week and requires manager approval.

Annual leave entitlement ranges from 14 to 26 days depending on seniority.
Leave requests must be entered into the HR system at least 5 business days
in advance. Maternity leave is 16 weeks, paternity leave is 10 days.

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Review
outcomes are the primary input for promotion and bonus decisions. Employees
may appeal review results within 5 business days.
